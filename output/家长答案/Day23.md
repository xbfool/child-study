# Day 23 · when / what time / what day / what date

## A 今日练习

1. **What time**

   订正：问钟点。

2. **What day**

   订正：问星期几。

3. **What date**

   订正：问日期。

4. **What time does Tom get up?**

   订正：does 后 get 用原形，去掉 at six。

5. **When do they play football?**

   订正：When + do + 主语。

6. **What day is it?**

   订正：星期用 What day。

7. **What date is it?**

   订正：日期用 What date。

8. **What time is it?**

   订正：问现在几点。

9. **What time does Lucy go to school?**

   订正：does 后用 go。

10. **When does Ben have a music lesson?**

   订正：has 还原为 have。

## B 订正后换句再试

1. **What time does Lucy go to bed?**

   订正：does + go。

2. **What day is it?**

   订正：问星期。
