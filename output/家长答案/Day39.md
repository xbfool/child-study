# Day 39 · 综合卷 A：不看提示

## A 今日练习

1. **Are the children in the park?**

   订正：children 复数，用 are。

2. **Can Tom speak English?**

   订正：can 提前。

3. **Does Lucy watch TV after dinner?**

   订正：watches 还原。

4. **Ben doesn't have a red bike.**

   订正：has 还原。

5. **How many ducks are there in the water?**

   订正：问数量，ducks 用复数。

6. **Whose book is that?**

   订正：问归属。

7. **How does Lily go to school?**

   订正：问方式，go 用原形。

8. **Don't let him touch the box.**

   订正：Don't let + 宾语 + 原形。

9. **What an interesting film it is!**

   订正：interesting 前用 an。

10. **How well the girls dance!**

   订正：well 修饰 dance，陈述语序。

## B 订正后换句再试

1. **How often does Ben play football?**

   订正：问频率。

2. **Is the girl making a card?**

   订正：进行时把 is 提前。
