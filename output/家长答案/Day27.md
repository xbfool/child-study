# Day 27 · why / how：原因、方式与天气

## A 今日练习

1. **Why**

   订正：because 回答原因。

2. **How**

   订正：问出行方式。

3. **How does Ben go to school?**

   订正：问方式，does + go。

4. **How do the children go to the park?**

   订正：复数主语用 do。

5. **Why is Lucy sad?**

   订正：why 代替整个原因部分。

6. **Why does Tom like winter?**

   订正：why + does，likes 还原。

7. **How is the weather today?**

   订正：问天气状态。

8. **What is the weather like today?**

   订正：What…like 是完整天气问法。

9. **Why does Ben like summer?**

   订正：does 后用原形。

10. **Because I run a lot.**

   订正：why 要回答原因。

## B 订正后换句再试

1. **How does Lily go to the library?**

   订正：方式用 How。

2. **Why is the boy happy?**

   订正：原句有 is，直接用 is 组问句。
