Day 17 · 每天做，和正在做

A 今日练习

1. plays

订正：习惯动作，单数主语用 plays。

2. is playing

订正：眼前正在踢球。

3. sing

订正：复数主语的习惯动作用原形。

4. are singing

订正：正在唱歌，are + singing。

5. Does Lucy read every day?

订正：一般现在时用 does。

6. Is Lucy reading now?

订正：现在进行时用 is。

7. Ben doesn't swim every Saturday.

订正：doesn't 后用 swim。

8. Ben isn't swimming now.

订正：is 后加 not。

9. She is making a cake now.

订正：makes 变 is making。

10. He washes his hands every day.

订正：is washing 变 washes。

B 订正后换句再试

1. Does the boy walk to school every day?

订正：does + walk。

2. Is the boy walking to school now?

订正：is + 主语 + walking。

C 旧题复练

1. are

订正：boys 配 are。

2. The boys don't skate every Sunday.

订正：复数主语，don't + 原形。
