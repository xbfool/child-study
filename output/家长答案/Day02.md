# Day 02 · be：肯定与否定

## A 今日练习

1. **She is not happy.**

   订正：在 is 后加 not；isn't 也对。

2. **They are not in the classroom.**

   订正：在 are 后加 not；aren't 也对。

3. **I am not tired.**

   订正：I 不需自动改成 you。

4. **He isn't tall.**

   订正：is not 缩写成 isn't。

5. **We aren't late.**

   订正：are not 缩写成 aren't。

6. **It is not cold.**

   订正：isn't = is not。

7. **aren't**

   订正：boys 是复数。

8. **Lucy is not sad.**

   订正：not 放在 is 后。

9. **The bag is not blue.**

   订正：这里用 is 的否定，不加 doesn't。

10. **The flowers are red.**

   订正：去掉 not，保留 are。

## B 订正后换句再试

1. **Ben is not at home.**

   订正：is 后加 not；isn't 也对。

2. **I am not hungry.**

   订正：am not 没有 amn't 这种标准缩写。
