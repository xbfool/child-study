# Day 24 · how / how old / how many

## A 今日练习

1. **How**

   订正：fine 回答状态。

2. **How old**

   订正：nine 在此回答年龄。

3. **How old is Lucy?**

   订正：去掉整个年龄信息。

4. **How are the children?**

   订正：how 问状态。

5. **How many books are there on the desk?**

   订正：How many + books + are there。

6. **How many sisters does Tom have?**

   订正：has 还原为 have。

7. **How many balls do they have?**

   订正：balls 用复数。

8. **How many books does Lucy have?**

   订正：how many 后名词用复数。

9. **How old is your brother?**

   订正：How old + is + 主语。

10. **There are two.**

   订正：要回答数量。

## B 订正后换句再试

1. **How many girls are there in the room?**

   订正：问数量而不是人是谁。

2. **How old is Lucy's sister?**

   订正：整个主语保留，问年龄。
