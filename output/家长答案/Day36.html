Day 36 · 混合练习一：先说思路，再动笔

A 今日练习

1. Is the girl in the room?

订正：be 结构，is 提前。

2. Can the girl make a cake?

订正：can 提前，make 不变。

3. Does the girl make cakes on Sunday?

订正：does + make。

4. Tom doesn't have a dog.

订正：has 还原为 have。

5. Where is Ben reading?

订正：地点用 Where，进行时用 is。

6. How many pencils does Lucy have?

订正：问数量，has 还原。

7. Don't touch the flowers.

订正：Don't + 原形。

8. Let him go home.

订正：let + 宾格 + 原形。

9. What a nice picture it is!

订正：What + 单数名词短语。

10. How well the boy swims!

订正：How + 副词 + 主语 + 动词。

B 订正后换句再试

1. What time does Tom go to school?

订正：does 后用 go。

2. Has Lily got a red bag?

订正：has 提前。

C 旧题复练

1. What beautiful flowers they are!

订正：flowers 复数，去掉 a。

2. Who can swim?

订正：who 代替 Tom，保留 can。
