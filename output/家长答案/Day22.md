# Day 22 · who / whose：谁，还是谁的

## A 今日练习

1. **Who**

   订正：问那个人是谁。

2. **Whose**

   订正：问包属于谁。

3. **Who is she?**

   订正：问身份，用 Who。

4. **Whose bag is this?**

   订正：Whose + bag，is this。

5. **Whose books are these?**

   订正：books 是复数，配 are。

6. **Whose pen is that?**

   订正：her 表示归属。

7. **It's Lily's.**

   订正：whose 要回答主人。

8. **He's Tom.**

   订正：who 要回答人是谁。

9. **Whose bag is this?**

   订正：who's = who is，不是 whose。

10. **Whose shoes are these?**

   订正：Whose + 名词 + are + these。

## B 订正后换句再试

1. **Whose coat is this?**

   订正：询问归属。

2. **Who is he?**

   订正：询问身份。
