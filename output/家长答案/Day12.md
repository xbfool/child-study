# Day 12 · have：用 do / does 提问和否定

## A 今日练习

1. **Do they have a ball?**

   订正：have 作普通动词。

2. **Does Lucy have a bike?**

   订正：has 还原为 have。

3. **Ben doesn't have a kite.**

   订正：doesn't 后用 have。

4. **The boys don't have a dog.**

   订正：boys 配 don't。

5. **Does she have breakfast at home?**

   订正：has breakfast 中 has 也还原。

6. **Yes, she does.**

   订正：does 问，does 答。

7. **No, they don't.**

   订正：Do 问，don't 答。

8. **Does Ben have a ruler?**

   订正：does 后不能保留 has。

9. **She doesn't have lunch at school.**

   订正：doesn't 后用 have。

10. **He has a pet.**

   订正：去掉 doesn't 后，have 变 has。

## B 订正后换句再试

1. **Does the cat have a long tail?**

   订正：has 变 have。

2. **My mother doesn't have tea after lunch.**

   订正：否定用 doesn't have。
