Day 09 · don’t / doesn’t：普通动词的否定句

A 今日练习

1. don't

订正：I 配 don't。

2. doesn't

订正：brother 是单数。

3. They don't read books every day.

订正：普通动词前加 don't。

4. Lucy doesn't read books every day.

订正：reads 回到 read。

5. Ben doesn't study Chinese.

订正：studies 回到 study。

6. The dog doesn't eat rice.

订正：eats 回到 eat。

7. He doesn't like bananas.

订正：doesn't 后不保留 s。

8. They don't play football.

订正：这里是普通动词的一般现在时。

9. She watches TV on Monday.

订正：去掉 doesn't 后，watch 恢复三单。

10. No, I don't.

订正：问 you，回答 I。

B 订正后换句再试

1. My father doesn't go to work by car.

订正：goes 还原成 go。

2. The boy washes his hands before lunch.

订正：单数主语，wash 加 es。

C 旧题复练

1. No, they don't.

订正：Do 问，don't 否定回答。

2. They are not in the classroom.

订正：在 are 后加 not；aren't 也对。
