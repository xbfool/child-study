Day 40 · 综合卷 B：隔天换句再检查

A 今日练习

1. goes

订正：单数主语的一般现在时。

2. Has Ben got a robot?

订正：has got 结构。

3. The children aren't running now.

订正：are 后加 not。

4. No, she doesn't.

订正：does 问，doesn't 答。

5. Who likes cats?

订正：who 作主语。

6. Why is Tom happy?

订正：原因用 Why，be 结构用 is。

7. How much are the shoes?

订正：问价格，复数用 are。

8. Let's not swim in the river.

订正：Let's not + 原形。

9. What beautiful pictures they are!

订正：复数不用 a。

10. How cute the dog is!

订正：the dog 改作主语。

B 订正后换句再试

1. What time does Ben have lunch?

订正：has 还原为 have。

2. How many books does Lucy have?

订正：数量用 How many，has 还原。

C 旧题复练

1. Are the children in the park?

订正：children 复数，用 are。

2. How kind Lucy is!

订正：How 后不加 a。
