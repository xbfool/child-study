Day 18 · 改单复数：整句一起检查

A 今日练习

1. These are red apples.

订正：this / is / a / apple 都要检查。

2. Those are eggs.

订正：that 变 those，去掉 an。

3. This is a book.

订正：these 变 this，补 a。

4. That is an orange bag.

订正：orange 以元音音素开头，用 an。

5. They have a kite.

订正：只指定换主语，a kite 不必变复数。

6. They play football.

订正：plays 变 play。

7. He can ride bikes.

订正：只换主语，can ride 不变。

8. The children are happy.

订正：child 的复数是 children。

9. The mice have long tails.

订正：本题改成多只老鼠，have，尾巴用复数。

10. These are books.

订正：these 配 are，复数名词前去掉 a。

B 订正后换句再试

1. These are big boxes.

订正：box 复数加 es。

2. She goes to school by bus.

订正：go 变 goes。

C 旧题复练

1. Does Lucy read every day?

订正：一般现在时用 does。

2. My brother has a football.

订正：have 的三单是 has。
