# Day 26 · which 和 what 的各种搭配

## A 今日练习

1. **Which**

   订正：在两个包中选择。

2. **What colour is the kite?**

   订正：问颜色。

3. **What colour are the shoes?**

   订正：shoes 是复数。

4. **What subject does Lucy like?**

   订正：subject 问学科，does + like。

5. **What size shoes does Ben wear?**

   订正：问鞋的尺码，wear 用原形。

6. **Which bag does Tom like?**

   订正：有给定的选择范围。

7. **kind of**

   订正：What kind of animals 问动物种类。

8. **What about some juice?**

   订正：What about + 名词短语。

9. **playing**

   订正：about 后接动词 ing。

10. **What colour is the bag?**

   订正：原句结构是 bag is，不能加 does。

## B 订正后换句再试

1. **What colour are the flowers?**

   订正：问颜色，用 are。

2. **How about playing a game?**

   订正：How about 后也接动词 ing。

## C 旧题复练

1. **How much is this book?**

   订正：问价格用 How much。 来源：Day 25 A9

2. **Don't run in the classroom.**

   订正：Don't + run。 来源：Day 19 A3
