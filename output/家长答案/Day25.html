Day 25 · how much：多少，还是多少钱

A 今日练习

1. much

订正：water 是不可数名词。

2. many

订正：apples 可数复数。

3. much

订正：rice 不可数。

4. How much is the bag?

订正：问价格，单数物品配 is。

5. How much are the shoes?

订正：shoes 复数配 are。

6. How much milk does Lucy have?

订正：问牛奶的量，用 How much milk。

7. How much juice is there in the bottle?

订正：问不可数名词的量。

8. How many eggs are there in the box?

订正：eggs 可数，用 How many。

9. How much is this book?

订正：问价格用 How much。

10. How many bananas does Ben have?

订正：bananas 可数复数。

B 订正后换句再试

1. How much is the toy car?

订正：问单个物品价格。

2. much

订正：bread 不可数。

C 旧题复练

1. How many sisters does Tom have?

订正：has 还原为 have。

2. These are books.

订正：these 配 are，复数名词前去掉 a。
