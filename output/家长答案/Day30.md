# Day 30 · 小复习四：疑问词与结构一起选

## A 今日练习

1. **Where is the dog?**

   订正：地点用 Where，be 结构用 is。

2. **Whose coat is this?**

   订正：问归属。

3. **What does Tom like?**

   订正：问喜欢什么，does + like。

4. **How old is Ben?**

   订正：问年龄。

5. **How many books are there on the desk?**

   订正：问数量，books 用复数。

6. **How much is the dress?**

   订正：问价格。

7. **What time does Lucy get up?**

   订正：问钟点，gets 还原。

8. **Why does Tom walk to school?**

   订正：问原因，walks 还原。

9. **Who has a pet?**

   订正：who 作主语不加 does。

10. **How often does Lily read books?**

   订正：问频率；When does Lily read books? 也可表达时间询问，但本页优先频率问法。

## B 订正后换句再试

1. **What colour are the shoes?**

   订正：问颜色。

2. **How does Lucy go to the park?**

   订正：问方式。

## C 旧题复练

1. **Who likes apples?**

   订正：谁喜欢？who 代替主语，likes 保留。 来源：Day 29 A1

2. **When do they play football?**

   订正：When + do + 主语。 来源：Day 23 A5
