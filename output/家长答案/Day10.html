Day 10 · 小复习二：be / can / do 三路混合

A 今日练习

1. Is Lucy a pupil?

订正：be 结构，is 提前。

2. Can Lucy draw?

订正：can 提前。

3. Does Lucy draw cats?

订正：普通动词，does + 原形。

4. The boys are not tired.

订正：are 后加 not。

5. The boys can't skate.

订正：can 后加 not。

6. The boys don't skate every Sunday.

订正：复数主语，don't + 原形。

7. walks

订正：普通动词肯定句用三单。

8. Is he ten?

订正：is 提问，不加 does。

9. Can she play football?

订正：can 后用原形。

10. No, he doesn't.

订正：Does 问，doesn't 答。

B 订正后换句再试

1. Does the bird sing every morning?

订正：sings 还原为 sing。

2. The bird can't sing.

订正：用 can 的否定。

C 旧题复练

1. don't

订正：I 配 don't。

2. No, they aren't.

订正：boys 用 they。
