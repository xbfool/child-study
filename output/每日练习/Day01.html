Day 01 · 先找“谁”，再选 am / is / are

姓名：____________ 日期：____________ 用时：______

先圈出句子在说谁。I 配 am；he / she / it、一个人或物配 is；you / we / they、复数配 are。you 指一个人时也配 are。

例子：My sister is happy. → My sister 是一个人，相当于 she，所以用 is。

A 今日练习

1. 圈出主语（句子在说谁）：The little girl is happy.
2. 圈出主语：Tom and Ben are friends.
3. 填 am / is / are：I ___ nine.
4. 填 am / is / are：You ___ my friend.
5. 填 am / is / are：My father ___ tall.
6. 填 am / is / are：The cats ___ small.
7. 用 he / she / it / they 替换主语：My mother is a teacher.
8. 用代词替换主语：The red bag is new.
9. 用代词替换主语：Lucy and Lily are happy.
10. 改正错误：My brother are ten.

B 订正后换句再试

1. 填 am / is / are：The boys ___ in the room.
2. 用代词替换主语：The dog is hungry.

C 旧题复练

1. 填 am / is / are：I ___ nine.
2. 用代词替换主语：Lucy and Lily are happy.
