Day 25 · how much：多少，还是多少钱

姓名：____________ 日期：____________ 用时：______

how much + 不可数名词可以问量；How much is / are…? 可以问价格。how many 后接可数名词复数。钱的多少与物品的数量要分清。

例子：How much milk is there? / How much are the shoes? / How many shoes are there?

A 今日练习

1. 填 many / much：How ___ water is there?
2. 填 many / much：How ___ apples are there?
3. 填 many / much：How ___ rice does Tom have?
4. 对画线部分提问：The bag is fifty yuan.
5. 对画线部分提问：The shoes are eighty yuan.
6. 对画线部分提问：Lucy has some milk.
7. 对画线部分提问：There is some juice in the bottle.
8. 对画线部分提问：There are six eggs in the box.
9. 改正错误：How many is this book?（问价格）
10. 改正错误：How much bananas does Ben have?

B 订正后换句再试

1. 对画线部分提问：The toy car is thirty yuan.
2. 填 many / much：How ___ bread is there?

C 旧题复练

1. 对画线部分提问：Tom has two sisters.
2. 改正错误：These is a books.

A 区：独立对 ___ / 10；看提示对 ___；待订正 ___。
订正：说意思 → 找主语 → 选结构 → 完整重写 → 换句再试。
