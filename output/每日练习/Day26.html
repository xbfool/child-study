Day 26 · which 和 what 的各种搭配

姓名：____________ 日期：____________ 用时：______

which 常在给定范围里选“哪一个”；what 常问“什么”。what colour / size / subject / kind of… 分别问颜色、尺码、学科、种类。固定词组要整体理解。

例子：The red bag or the blue bag? → Which bag…? / The bag is red. → What colour…?

A 今日练习

1. 选词：___ bag do you like, the red one or the blue one? (Which / Where)
2. 对画线部分提问：The kite is yellow.
3. 对画线部分提问：The shoes are black.
4. 对画线部分提问，用 What subject：Lucy likes English.
5. 对画线部分提问：Ben wears size 32 shoes.
6. 对画线部分提问，用 Which bag：Tom likes the blue bag, not the red one.
7. 填空：What ___ animals does Lucy like? She likes cats.（问种类）
8. 用 What about 提建议：“喝些果汁怎么样？”
9. 选词：What about (play / playing) football?
10. 改正错误：What colour does the bag is?

B 订正后换句再试

1. 对画线部分提问：The flowers are pink.
2. 用 How about 改写同义句：What about playing a game?

C 旧题复练

1. 改正错误：How many is this book?（问价格）
2. 改为否定祈使句：Run in the classroom.

A 区：独立对 ___ / 10；看提示对 ___；待订正 ___。
订正：说意思 → 找主语 → 选结构 → 完整重写 → 换句再试。
