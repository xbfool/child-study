Day 14 · there be：某处有；some / any 看语境

姓名：____________ 日期：____________ 用时：______

there is / are 表示“某处有”；人“拥有”通常用 have / has。there be 提问把 is / are 提前，简答用 there。普通询问或否定中 some 常改 any；请求、邀请中可保留 some。

例子：There are some books on the desk. → Are there any books on the desk?

A 今日练习

1. 填 is / are：There ___ a cat under the table.
2. 填 is / are：There ___ three books in the bag.
3. 填 is / are：There ___ some milk in the glass.
4. 选词：Tom (has / there is) a red bag.
5. 改为一般疑问句：There is a ball in the box.
6. 改为一般疑问句（普通询问）：There are some apples on the table.
7. 改为否定句：There is some water in the bottle.
8. 作肯定简短回答：Are there any books on the desk?
9. 作否定简短回答：Is there a dog in the room?
10. 选词：Would you like (some / any) milk?（邀请对方喝一点）

B 订正后换句再试

1. 改为一般疑问句（普通询问）：There is some juice in the cup.
2. 改为否定句：There are some birds in the tree.

C 旧题复练

1. 改为一般疑问句，保留 got：Lucy has got a doll.
2. 写出第三人称单数形式：do
