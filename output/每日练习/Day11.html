Day 11 · have / has：谁有，谁吃，谁上课

姓名：____________ 日期：____________ 用时：______

一般现在时肯定句中，I / you / we / they 用 have；he / she / it 等第三人称单数用 has。have 可以表示“有”，也出现在 have breakfast、have a lesson 等搭配中。

例子：They have a ball. / She has a ball. / She has breakfast at seven.

A 今日练习

1. 选词：I (have / has) a new bag.
2. 选词：You (have / has) a red pen.
3. 选词：Tom (have / has) a bike.
4. 选词：The girls (have / has) long hair.
5. 用所给词填空：My father ___ breakfast at seven. (have)
6. 用所给词填空：We ___ an English lesson on Monday. (have)
7. 用 She 改写：They have a cat.
8. 用 They 改写：He has two books.
9. 改正错误：My brother haves a football.
10. 改正错误：She can has some milk now.

B 订正后换句再试

1. 用所给词填空：The dog ___ a long tail. (have)
2. 用 We 改写：She has lunch at school.

C 旧题复练

1. 改为否定句：The boys are tired.
2. 改正错误：Does Lucy can swim?

A 区：独立对 ___ / 10；看提示对 ___；待订正 ___。
订正：说意思 → 找主语 → 选结构 → 完整重写 → 换句再试。
