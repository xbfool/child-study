Day 27 · why / how：原因、方式与天气

姓名：____________ 日期：____________ 用时：______

why 问原因，常用 because 回答；how 问方式，可以用 by bus / on foot 等回答。How is the weather? 与 What is the weather like? 都可以问天气。

例子：He walks to school. → How does he go to school? / He is happy because… → Why is he happy?

A 今日练习

1. 选词：___ is Lucy happy? Because it is her birthday. (Why / Where)
2. 选词：___ does Tom go to school? By bus. (How / How old)
3. 对画线部分提问：Ben goes to school by bike.
4. 对画线部分提问：The children go to the park on foot.
5. 对画线部分提问：Lucy is sad because her cat is ill.
6. 对画线部分提问：Tom likes winter because he can make a snowman.
7. 按天气提问，用 How：It is sunny today.
8. 改写同义问句，用 What：How is the weather today?
9. 改正错误：Why does Ben likes summer?
10. 选择符合问题的回答：Why are you tired? (By bus. / Because I run a lot.)

B 订正后换句再试

1. 对画线部分提问：Lily goes to the library by bus.
2. 对画线部分提问：The boy is happy because he has a new bike.

C 旧题复练

1. 对画线部分提问：The kite is yellow.
2. 改为否定祈使句：Let him open the box.

A 区：独立对 ___ / 10；看提示对 ___；待订正 ___。
订正：说意思 → 找主语 → 选结构 → 完整重写 → 换句再试。
