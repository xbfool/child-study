Day 12 · have：用 do / does 提问和否定

姓名：____________ 日期：____________ 用时：______

本页练不带 got 的 have，统一使用 do / does 这套：Does she have…? / She doesn’t have…；have breakfast 也按普通动词处理。

例子：He has a bike. → Does he have a bike? → He doesn’t have a bike.

A 今日练习

1. 改为一般疑问句（用 Do）：They have a ball.
2. 改为一般疑问句（用 Does）：Lucy has a bike.
3. 改为否定句（用 doesn't）：Ben has a kite.
4. 改为否定句（用 don't）：The boys have a dog.
5. 改为一般疑问句：She has breakfast at home.
6. 作肯定简短回答：Does Lucy have a sister?
7. 作否定简短回答：Do the boys have a ball?
8. 改正错误：Does Ben has a ruler?
9. 改正错误：She doesn't has lunch at school.
10. 改为肯定句：He doesn't have a pet.

B 订正后换句再试

1. 改为一般疑问句（用 Does）：The cat has a long tail.
2. 改为否定句（用 doesn't）：My mother has tea after lunch.

C 旧题复练

1. 用 She 改写：They have a cat.
2. 填空：The children ___ happy. (am / is / are)

A 区：独立对 ___ / 10；看提示对 ___；待订正 ___。
订正：说意思 → 找主语 → 选结构 → 完整重写 → 换句再试。
