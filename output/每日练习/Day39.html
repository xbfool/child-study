A 今日练习

1. 改为一般疑问句：The children are in the park.
2. 改为一般疑问句：Tom can speak English.
3. 改为一般疑问句：Lucy watches TV after dinner.
4. 改为否定句（用 doesn't）：Ben has a red bike.
5. 对画线部分提问：There are eight ducks in the water.
6. 对画线部分提问，保留 book：That is Tom's book.
7. 对画线部分提问：Lily goes to school on foot.
8. 改为否定祈使句：Let him touch the box.
9. 用 What 改为完整感叹句：It is an interesting film.
10. 用 How 改为完整感叹句：The girls dance well.
